# Roadmap

What is planned. Nothing here is built yet, and nothing here is confirmed to work on the car unless it says so. For
each item the first step is to find out on the car what it needs, before promising it.

## Features planned

| Feature | What it would do | Where it would live | First step | Care |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Maintenance Schedule** | Keeps track of past and upcoming maintenance: what was done and when, and what is due next. | A menu of its own, with a switch in Setting to turn it on or off. | Decide what an entry holds (date, odometer, what was done, and when the next one is due, by date or by distance). The odometer is already recorded with every trip. Entries would stay on the device, like the trips, and be part of the backup. | None to speak of: it only keeps notes and does not control the car. |

## How an item moves

An item stays here until it is built. When it is, it gets a section in the [README](../README.md) and leaves this
list. An item that turns out not to be possible on the car is removed with a line saying why, so it is not tried
again.
